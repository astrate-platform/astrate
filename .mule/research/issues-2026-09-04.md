### unregistered-device-transient-trigger
Title: Keep the Channels `watch` path tolerant of unregistered devices (watch note, not a task)
Scope: design-decision
Body:
Not a candidate — there is nothing to change today. Upstream `astarte-platform` commit `662d4f1`
("feat: Allow volatile trigger installation for not yet registered devices", 2026-08-31) explicitly
unblocked installing transient (volatile) triggers for devices that are not yet registered. Astrate
`internal/appengine/channels/ws.go:280-317` (handleWatch) and `room.go:115-137` (Room.Watch) already
accept a `watch` for an unregistered device — they check only the WATCH authorization path, never
registration — so Astrate is already a superset of the newly-enabled upstream behaviour. This is a
deliberate note for whoever later touches the watch path: if a registration check is ever added there
(they are upstream and Astrate are not currently aligned on that axis), it must stay tolerant of
unregistered devices or it would regress into a deviation from upstream's now-documented intent.
