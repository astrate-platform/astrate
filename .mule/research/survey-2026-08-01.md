protocol, which is the survey's focus.

### Channels / live bus

against the self-hosted upstream (`test/conformance/upstream/channels.json`); Astrate
now reads `a_ch` by upstream's exact-verb rule, enforces `JOIN` on joins, and
authorizes a data-trigger watch against `<device_id>/<interface_name><match_path>`
and a device-trigger watch against the bare `<device_id>` (`COMPATIBILITY.md`
deviation 1). The **group** WATCH shapes remain unmeasured (no group in the realm).

### Code-vs-docs disagreement

## Source 3 — upstream `astarte-platform` (5047681, unchanged)

No new commits since the 07-30 survey. Re-verified against a fresh shallow clone:

- **Malformed capabilities → force-disconnect.** `capabilities_handler.ex:74-81`
  builds a `DeviceErrorEvent` with `error_name: "malformed_capabilities_message"`
  and hands it to `Error.handle_error`, whose `ask_clean_session` defaults to `true`
  (`error.ex:79-87`) → `Core.Device.ask_clean_session/2` →
  `force_disconnection(realm, device_id)` (`device.ex:322-347`). The protocol doc
  states the contract (`080-mqtt-v1-protocol.md:125-128`): malformed message →
  previously stored values unchanged + device forcefully disconnected.
- **Capabilities are persisted.** `090-database.md:123-129` defines a `capabilities`
  UDT on the `devices` table; `Queries.set_device_capabilities/3`
  (`queries.ex:527`) writes the merged struct on every valid message. Doc line 128:
  "Capabilities are preserved in the database, ensuring consistency across sessions."
- **`unset_not_allowed` error_name confirmed** (`data_handler.ex:255-260`), as cited
  in the 07-30 candidate.
- Key-agreement error names (`key_agreement_transition_error`, `exchange_failed_error`,
  …) still present in `control_handler.ex` as before.

Nothing else moved upstream; no new interface, protocol, or trigger behaviour since
`5047681` (which the last two runs already covered).
