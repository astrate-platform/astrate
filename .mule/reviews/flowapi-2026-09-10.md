## What I found

The package is well-built and unusually well-documented: restart dedupe
(`restartMu`/`restarting`), ErrValidation wrapping, and the lazy-store
short-circuits in `checkBlockTypes`/`expandStoredComposites` are all sound
(no nil-path, no context-ignored store call). The `Store.Ingest`/`register`
plumbing tests are honest. Two real behavioural gaps, both in the auto-restart
goroutine, plus two untested wire rules.

1. **Auto-restart retries forever on a permanent failure.**
   `restartWithBackoff` (service.go:585-621) has no terminal condition other
   than success or flow deletion. If `RestartFlowInstance` fails with a
   permanent error — pipeline deleted (`resolveAndBuild` wraps store.ErrNotFound
   as ErrValidation, service.go:360-363), config that no longer substitutes, a
   definition that no longer parses — the durable row is flipped to "failed"
   (service.go:494-497) but `AutoRestart` stays true, the loop reads it again,
   and it retries every ≤30s **forever**: DB reads + a failed-graph build + a
   failed status write + a Warn log, each cycle. No exit short of deleting the
   flow. A flow whose pipeline was deleted is a zombie that hot-loops on dead
   config.
2. **The restart goroutine has no shutdown path.** `onBlockFatal` fires
   `go s.restartWithBackoff(...)` (service.go:560) with no stop signal. At
   process shutdown (main.go:468-491) the flow `Manager.Shutdown` drains
   running flows, but nothing cancels the goroutine; a block-death racing
   shutdown can awake in the loop, call `mgr.StartFlow` on the emptied manager
   (whose pump runs on `context.Background()`, flow.go:173-175), and flip the
   durable status back to "running" after the shutdown sequence completed.
3. **`validationDetail` (http.go:297-304), untested.** The layer that turns
   `ErrValidation` into the 422 detail string has no test at all. Its exact
   edge — a message that *equals* the `"flowapi: validation failed: "` prefix
   is **not** stripped (the guard is `len(msg) > len(prefix)`) — would regress
   silently.
4. **POST /flows auto_restart default, untested.** `startFlow` defaults a
   missing `auto_restart` to `true` (http.go:141-144) — a real wire rule with
   no test. A regression to `false` would pass every existing suite.

## What I decided NOT to propose, and why

- **`DeletePipeline` (service.go:199-206) silently removes a pipeline
  referencing flows still reference it** — update's `ReferencingFlows` (issue
  #44) proves the intent was transparency, and deleting a referenced pipeline
  is what makes finding 1 retry forever. Escalated to `.mule/for-giulio.md`
  instead of queued: refuse-vs-warn-and-allow is a product decision, and
  verifying either needs a store (interface refactor or `[legion]`).
- **`userBlockResolver` reloads `ListUserBlocks` once per composite name**
  (userblocks.go:161-186) — N store round-trips per N-reference source. Perf
  without a named workload; composites are small and rare. Bench-first if ever
  pursued.
- **`liveFlow` and `referencingFlows` linear-scan per call** (service.go:186-
  197, 887-895) — O(flows) per request. Fine at operator-scale flow counts.
- **`mergeFlowView` "{}" empty-config default, `mustJSON`, `nilIfEmptyJSON`**
  (service.go:925-928, 958-964; userblocks.go:204-209) — defensive, unreachable
  from current callers (config is normalized to `{}` before insert). Not worth
  a line.
- **Restart loop vs manual reload races** — the `restarting` map already
  dedupes (service.go:38-41, 566-583); a double `StartFlow` is resolved by
  `ErrFlowExists`. Transient, handled.

## Result

Four proposals, naming their files and the rule each pins. This was a proposal
