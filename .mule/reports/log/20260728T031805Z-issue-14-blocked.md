`/<realm>/<device_id>/control/keyAgreement` topic for a CBOR-payload key-agreement handshake.

implemented" in upstream either, so Astrate and upstream are aligned in not supporting it.

implicitly (it is not in the allow-list), rather than as a documented, tested decision.
